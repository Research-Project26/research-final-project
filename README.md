

\# Cybersecurity Awareness Research



\## Project Overview

This research studies university students' cybersecurity knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors (KAB) using a questionnaire.



\## Objectives

\- Assess students' cybersecurity awareness.

\- Examine their attitudes and security practices.

\- Identify areas for improving cybersecurity awareness.



\## Folder Structure

\- `proposal/` – Research proposal

\- `instruments/` – Questionnaire

\- `pilot-study/` – Pilot testing

\- `data/` – Research dataset

\- `analysis/` – Data analysis

\- `report/` – Final report

\- `presentation/` – Presentation slides

\- `meeting-notes/` – Group meeting notes

\- `references/` – Research references



\## Tools

Python, Pandas, Jupyter Notebook, Google Forms, and statistical software.
